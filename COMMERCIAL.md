# Commercial use

**If you are a company, or you are being paid to produce videos with this software, you need a
commercial license.**

The free PolyForm Noncommercial grant does not cover you. That is not a loophole, and it is not
going to be waived retroactively: the whole point of this licence is that commercial production is
the thing that pays.

## What counts as commercial

**Commercial — requires a license:**

- A company using autovideo to produce advertising, marketing, social, or promotional video
- A video produced as part of paid client work, or as a deliverable for a client
- A SaaS product, an agency, or a studio whose output is sold
- Anything produced with the intention of commercial advantage to a business
- Use inside a company that is itself a for-profit entity making video as part of its business

**Non-commercial — no license needed:**

- Personal projects, and videos you make for yourself
- Learning, teaching, coursework, research
- Hobby and enthusiast work
- Open-source development, contributing back to autovideo itself
- Nonprofits, charities, universities, public research bodies, and government
- Evaluating autovideo before deciding whether to buy — including against a real application

## The line, in one sentence

**If the video you make with this is something you sell, or something that earns money for a
business, it is commercial.**

## Why this exists

autovideo exists because turning a real application into a watchable video takes an unreasonable
amount of fiddly work. The people who get the most out of it are often individuals, students and
open-source contributors, and they should not have to pay to use it. The people it is _most_ built
for, though, are companies producing marketing video — so that is who pays.

The split is intentional, and it is what keeps the free tier free.

## Getting a license

Open an issue on the repository, or email the address in the repository's front page, describing
what you want to use it for and at what scale. Licensing terms — scope, seats, support, price —
are agreed per engagement.

Until a license is agreed, the non-commercial terms in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) apply, and using the
software commercially without one is outside them.
